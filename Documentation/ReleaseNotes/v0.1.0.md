# Yap 0.1.0

For people who professionally yap for a living.

**Release preparation:** version 0.1.0, build 1. The installer remains a draft pending Zoom distribution approval. These notes are ready for the first release; no published artifact, successful production onboarding or Zoom approval is asserted here. Confirm the release audience and final packaging evidence before publication.

Yap brings live Zoom meetings, your agenda and past conversations into a native Mac app.

- Join from Google Calendar, paste a meeting link, or start a meeting with your connected Zoom account. Keep chat, people and screen-sharing controls close at hand.
- Find cloud recordings by title or date, load earlier months, stream a video or save an MP4. Reopening the library refreshes it without interrupting playback.
- Switch the video's layout without losing your place. Play at 1× through 2.75×, open a dedicated recording window, or go fullscreen.
- Follow saved chat and spoken transcripts alongside a recording. Search passages, click to seek, copy text, or export the transcript as TXT or WebVTT.
- Use Space to play/pause, arrows to move ten seconds, S for speed, V for layout and F for fullscreen.

## Install

Requires **Apple silicon and macOS 26 or newer**. The planned release assets are [Yap.dmg](https://github.com/grinich/yap/releases/download/v0.1.0/Yap.dmg) and [Yap-macOS.zip](https://github.com/grinich/yap/releases/download/v0.1.0/Yap-macOS.zip), with matching SHA-256 files and `appcast.xml`.

Open the DMG, drag Yap.app to Applications, and launch it there. Alternatively, expand the ZIP and move its Yap.app to Applications. Quit any previous app before switching. Your existing Yap preferences and saved accounts take precedence; supported older accounts/preferences can migrate through the normal Keychain permission flow. macOS permissions and existing Shortcuts may need approval or reselection.

## Availability

External Zoom approval and successful managed production onboarding remain pending. The source and SDK-free sample interface are available for exploration; personal developer setup remains subject to the provider's permitted use. The integrated installer must be released to the audience covered by the applicable Zoom permission. See [distribution status](https://github.com/grinich/yap/blob/main/Documentation/Distribution.md).

Cloud recordings, chat, transcripts and video layouts depend on the files Zoom saved and your account's permissions. Calendar is optional. This first version does not establish an end-to-end update from an earlier public Yap release; that requires a subsequent version.
