# Yap 0.1.0

For people who professionally yap for a living.

**Superseded historical candidate: 0.1.0, build 1. Do not install or promote this draft.** It predates the corrected icon and current managed authorization endpoint. Its immutable tag and signed assets are retained for traceability; the replacement [0.1.1 candidate](v0.1.1.md) is being prepared. The 0.1.0 app and DMG passed Apple notarization, stapling and Gatekeeper checks, and downloaded-artifact verification passed. Those checks apply only to 0.1.0, not to the replacement candidate. [Historical packaging evidence](../Releases.md#release-candidate-evidence).

Yap brings live Zoom meetings, your agenda and past conversations into a native Mac app.

- Join from Google Calendar, paste a meeting link, or start a meeting with your connected Zoom account. Keep chat, people and screen-sharing controls close at hand.
- Find cloud recordings by title or date, load earlier months, stream a video or save an MP4. Reopening the library refreshes it without interrupting playback.
- Switch the video's layout without losing your place. Play at 1× through 2.75×, open a dedicated recording window, or go fullscreen.
- Follow saved chat and spoken transcripts alongside a recording. Search passages, click to seek, copy text, or export the transcript as TXT or WebVTT.
- Use Space to play/pause, arrows to move ten seconds, S for speed, V for layout and F for fullscreen.

## Availability

This superseded candidate is not offered for installation. External Zoom approval and native acceptance of the replacement candidate remain pending. An in-memory production browser PKCE check passed consent/callback, token exchange with the required scopes, a bounded recordings-list request and the SDK-signature response; it did not test native GUI/Keychain integration, SDK initialization, refresh or revocation. The initial audience is outside the EU; the production listing has EU availability disabled. The source and SDK-free sample interface are available for exploration; personal developer setup remains subject to the provider's permitted use. The integrated installer must be released to the audience covered by the applicable Zoom permission. See [distribution status](https://github.com/grinich/yap/blob/main/Documentation/Distribution.md).

Cloud recordings, chat, transcripts and video layouts depend on the files Zoom saved and your account's permissions. Calendar is optional. This first version does not establish an end-to-end update from an earlier public Yap release; that requires a subsequent version.
