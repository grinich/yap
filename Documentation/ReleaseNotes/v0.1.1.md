# Yap 0.1.1

For people who professionally yap for a living.

**Release candidate: 0.1.1, build 2 — in preparation.** Build, signing, notarization and downloaded-artifact verification are pending for this version. It will remain a GitHub draft until native acceptance and Zoom distribution approval are complete. This is not a public release. The superseded 0.1.0 draft must not be installed or promoted; its successful notarization does not verify this candidate. [Packaging status](../Releases.md#first-release-v011).

Yap brings live Zoom meetings, your agenda and past conversations into a native Mac app.

- Join from Google Calendar, paste a meeting link, or start a meeting with your connected Zoom account. Keep chat, people and screen-sharing controls close at hand.
- Find cloud recordings by title or date, load earlier months, stream a video or save an MP4. Reopening the library refreshes it without interrupting playback.
- Switch the video's layout without losing your place. Play at 1× through 2.75×, open a dedicated recording window, or go fullscreen.
- Follow saved chat and spoken transcripts alongside a recording. Search passages, click to seek, copy text, or export the transcript as TXT or WebVTT.
- Use Space to play/pause, arrows to move ten seconds, S for speed, V for layout and F for fullscreen.

## Planned installation

Requires **Apple silicon and macOS 26 or newer**. The planned release assets are [Yap.dmg](https://github.com/grinich/yap/releases/download/v0.1.1/Yap.dmg) and [Yap-macOS.zip](https://github.com/grinich/yap/releases/download/v0.1.1/Yap-macOS.zip), their matching `.sha256` files and `appcast.xml`. These URLs are not current public downloads. [All five planned artifact links](../Releases.md#first-release-v011).

After approval and publication, open the DMG, drag Yap.app to Applications, and launch it there. Alternatively, expand the ZIP and move its Yap.app to Applications. Quit any previous app before switching. Keep the previous app until the replacement's account access is verified. Your existing Yap preferences and saved accounts take precedence; supported older accounts/preferences can migrate through the normal Keychain permission flow. macOS permissions and existing Shortcuts may need approval or reselection.

## Availability and verification

The initial audience is outside the EU; the production listing has EU availability disabled. Zoom submission and approval remain pending. The source and SDK-free sample interface are available for exploration; personal developer setup remains subject to the provider's permitted use. The integrated installer must be released only to an audience covered by applicable Zoom permission. See [distribution status](https://github.com/grinich/yap/blob/main/Documentation/Distribution.md).

An in-memory production browser PKCE check passed at the managed endpoint: consent/callback, token exchange with all three required scopes, one bounded recordings-list page and an SDK-signature response. This did not test native GUI/Keychain integration, SDK initialization, token refresh or revocation. Native installation acceptance and verification of the actual 0.1.1 app and installer remain pending.

Cloud recordings, chat, transcripts and video layouts depend on the files Zoom saved and your account's permissions. Calendar is optional. An end-to-end update from an earlier public Yap release has not been tested.
