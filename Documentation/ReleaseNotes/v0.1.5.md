# Yap 0.1.5

An alternative Zoom client for people who professionally yap for a living. Requires **Apple silicon and macOS 26 or newer**.

## What changed

- **A better picture-in-picture window.** Drag the video to reposition it, resize from any corner, or click it to return to the main meeting window. Microphone, camera, cloud recording, and leave/end-call controls are available directly in PiP. The video fills the window cleanly.
- **Consistent video-layout controls.** The live meeting menu now uses the same compact layout icons and dropdown style as the recording player.
- **Discoverable meeting shortcuts.** The main window shows ⇧⌘N to start a meeting and ⌘Return when joining the next eligible meeting is available.
- **A nicer Google Calendar sign-in finish.** The completion page now matches Yap, supports light and dark mode, and offers an Open Yap button. Successful sign-in brings the app forward after the connection is saved.

Includes the smaller Apple-silicon-only runtime, bundled Google sign-in client, recordings with synchronized chat and transcripts, and playback speeds up to 2.75× from previous releases.

## Install

Download **Yap.dmg**, open it, and drag Yap to Applications. Alternatively, expand **Yap-macOS.zip**. The release includes Developer ID signed, notarized installers, SHA-256 files, and a signed in-app update feed.

## Account availability

Zoom Marketplace review is still pending, so Zoom may restrict authorization and external meetings. Google Calendar sign-in is in production but unverified; Google’s warning and user cap apply until approval. Calendar is optional. Publishing this installer does not establish either provider’s approval. The 0.1.3 installer supplied to Zoom review remains unchanged.
