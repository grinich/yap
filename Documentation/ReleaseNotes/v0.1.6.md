# Yap 0.1.6

Your schedule in the menu bar, more expressive chat, and better camera and sharing controls. Requires **Apple silicon and macOS 26 or newer**.

## What changed

- **Your schedule in the menu bar.** See current and upcoming events, countdowns, today and tomorrow, all-day events, and earlier meetings. Join eligible Zoom meetings or open the event’s day in Google Calendar.
- **Camera effects inside Yap.** Choose background blur, add a photo background, or automatically frame your face, with a local preview in Settings. Availability follows Zoom and your hardware.
- **Native microphone, speaker, and camera menus.** Choose devices, adjust volume and microphone gain, and test your audio. Raise or lower your hand from the call controls when other people are present.
- **Expanded meeting chat.** Send private and waiting-room messages, reply in threads, quote and copy messages, use bold/italic/underline/strikethrough and links, and delete your own messages when allowed. Send and save files with progress, cancellation and download retry. Search chat or save a text transcript. Available recipients follow meeting permissions.
- **Chat notifications that stay out of the way.** Unread badges, a gentle pulse, and temporary message bubbles when chat is closed. Choose a notification sound in Settings or turn it off.
- **Group photos.** Choose Take photo from the layout menu for a countdown and a collage of active cameras, without names or camera-off tiles. Photos save to Downloads automatically. Larger groups are captured in batches of up to 49 cameras; the result is not a simultaneous snapshot of every batch.
- **Better screen sharing.** Share computer sound without a screen, switch between shared content and people, and keep your self-view, conference rooms, and recent speakers visible with an active-speaker highlight. Hide self-view or show non-video participants from the layout menu. Screen-sharing access is included in onboarding and can be retried after changing macOS settings.
- **Calendar-aware meetings and simpler sign-in.** Meetings show the matching event title and time interval. Connect Google Calendar starts sign-in directly; every build includes Yap’s shared Google app configuration. Improved native invitation handling fixes links that worked when pasted but failed when clicked.
- **Meeting and playback polish.** Keep a call window on top, move self-view between corners, access the invite link next to the title, and change video layout outside the chat/people pane. Improved settings controls, recording playback, and sign-in/error feedback.

## Install or update

Choose **Yap → Check for Updates…**, or download **Yap.dmg**, open it, and drag Yap to Applications. **Yap-macOS.zip** is the alternative archive. The release includes Developer ID signed and notarized installers, SHA-256 files, and a signed in-app update feed. Finish any active call before installing an update.

## Known limits

- Zoom’s public macOS SDK does not expose sent-message editing or per-message emoji reactions. Those commands are not available in Yap.
- Automatic nearby Zoom Room pairing remains experimental and is not confirmed functional. If pairing is unavailable, join the room’s meeting by link or ID before sharing.
- Camera effects, chat features, file transfers, and meeting access depend on Zoom, account permissions, and available hardware. Local preview tests do not establish interoperability with every Zoom meeting or client.
- Zoom and Google account availability remains subject to provider review and restrictions. This release does not establish either provider’s approval.
