# Yap 0.1.7

Faster meeting access, a saved Join quietly preference, and more room to view shared screens. Requires **Apple silicon and macOS 26 or newer**.

## What changed

- **Choose how you enter meetings.** Join quietly is now a saved switch in General settings, on by default. Turn it off to request your microphone and camera on when joining or hosting, subject to macOS permissions and meeting restrictions. Changing it leaves your current call unchanged. Sharing to a room still starts with audio disconnected and video off.
- **Join directly from the menu bar.** Click an eligible Zoom event to join, without opening an event submenu. Events with multiple meeting links still offer a choice; non-Zoom events open their day in Google Calendar. During a call, the menu bar shows the current meeting’s name and a video icon.
- **Get closer to shared content.** Incoming screen shares fill more of the meeting window. Zoom from 1× to 4×, pan around details, and choose Fit to see the whole share again. These controls change only your view.
- **Steadier chat controls.** Fixed flickering Reply and More buttons when moving between messages and their actions. Open menus and keyboard focus keep their controls visible, while message text remains selectable.
- **Smoother account connections.** Updated Zoom sign-in and its return to Yap. Google Calendar sign-in now finishes on Yap-branded pages; cancelling no longer opens an error alert in Yap, and connection errors appear inline.
- **Clearer settings and cleaner quitting.** Finish editing your display name with Done, Return, or a click outside the field. Zoom developer configuration now lives in Development settings. Yap shuts down Zoom and camera effects before the app exits.

## Install or update

Choose **Yap → Check for Updates…**, or download **Yap.dmg**, open it, and drag Yap to Applications. **Yap-macOS.zip** is the alternative archive. Finish any active call before installing an update.

## Known limits

- Zoom and Google account availability remains subject to provider review and restrictions. This release does not establish either provider’s approval.
- Zoom’s public macOS SDK does not expose sent-message editing or per-message emoji reactions. Those commands remain unavailable.
- Automatic nearby Zoom Room pairing remains experimental. If pairing is unavailable, join the room’s meeting by link or ID before sharing.
- Meeting access, camera effects, chat, and file transfers depend on Zoom, account permissions, and available hardware.
