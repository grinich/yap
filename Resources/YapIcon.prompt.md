# Yap icon artwork

Finalized September 25, 2026 from the user-selected **A — Refined camera** concept. Generated and refined with the built-in image-generation tool.

The ivory camera and recessed rounded-square lens sit on a warm gold-to-orange gradient. The master in `Yap.icon/Assets/Yap-Art.png` is fully opaque and fills the square canvas. The Icon Composer layer uses scale 1 so the artwork reaches Apple's system mask without a second inset frame. Apple's asset compiler supplies the native outer silhouette, small-size renditions, and appearances; no rounded tile or outer shadow is baked into the master.

`Scripts/package-icon.sh` compiles the `.icon` document and exports `YapIcon.png` from its 256-pixel ICNS rendition, keeping the in-app, README, and website icon consistent. App builds compile the same source document directly into `Assets.car` and `Yap.icns`.
