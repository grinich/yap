# Yap icon artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original full-bleed square artwork is in `Yap.icon/Assets/Yap-Art.png`; Apple's Icon Composer pipeline applies the native macOS icon silhouette. `YapIcon.png` is the compiled icon's PNG representation for documentation and the website.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: final macOS app icon master for Yap, a native GROUP VIDEO CHAT application.
Primary request: A gorgeous icon showing a group of people's FACES IN A GRID, instantly recognizable as the familiar gallery view of a group video call. The video-chat grid IS the icon. Make this literal and beautifully executed, not an abstract logo.
Design: four distinctive, warm, expressive people in a precise TWO BY TWO grid of four equal square portrait tiles. Each tile contains one large, front-facing head and upper shoulders, as though looking into their own webcam. Four heads total: a smiling woman with a dark curly bob, a friendly man with close-cropped hair and rounded glasses, a woman with a short light haircut, and a man with soft wavy dark hair. Different skin tones and clothing give the small portraits strong visual distinction. These are sophisticated stylized 3D portraits, charming and expressive, with the polish of the best contemporary macOS icon design: softly sculpted features, tidy rounded hair shapes, friendly natural eyes, restrained detail, soft satin materials. Not photographs and not grotesque caricatures. One person can be visibly mid-conversation, another listening with a slight smile.
Each of the four portrait tiles has a different harmonious softly shaded color background: warm coral, periwinkle blue, pale mint, and golden apricot. Rich but tasteful colors. A thin, consistent deep graphite gutter separates the four equal tiles and frames their outside edges. Tiles have gently rounded corners, creating an impeccably aligned 2x2 gallery. Heads are large and consistent in scale, all fully visible with the top of the hair and shoulders comfortably framed. Clear silhouettes and simplified eyes/noses/mouths so the grid still reads at small Dock sizes. Almost front-on orthographic view; no tilted perspective. Gentle depth and clean lighting make the portraits feel tactile.
Composition: the 2x2 grid should occupy nearly the full square canvas with only a modest even outer margin, around 6%. Fill the canvas to every edge with the same deep graphite. The artwork has STRAIGHT square outer corners because Apple's icon compiler applies the native rounded-square outline. No surrounding canvas, no mockup of a computer screen, no external drop shadow, no text, no labels, no toolbar, no camera symbol, no speech bubbles, no quotation marks, no mouth-only mascot. ONE finished icon, four faces total, carefully balanced as a single cohesive video-call grid.

## Production cleanup prompt

Precise production cleanup of this video-chat grid icon. Preserve the four portraits, their composition, expressions, colors, and 2×2 tile arrangement. Repair the background and gutters only: eliminate ALL transparent areas, multicolored speckles, halos, streaks and ragged fragments surrounding the four portrait tiles. Replace those outer margins and the narrow vertical and horizontal gutters with a perfectly clean, solid, fully opaque dark charcoal color (#191B23). Give each portrait tile a crisp smooth rounded corner with precise antialiased edges. The outermost canvas must be a fully opaque square with charcoal filling every edge, no transparent pixels and no outside mockup. Preserve all four portraits and their tile backgrounds exactly. No extra graphics or text.
